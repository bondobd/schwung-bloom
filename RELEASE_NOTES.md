## Fixes

- **Move 2.1.1:** Schwung reports Move's transport as unknown on this firmware. Bloom no longer reads that as "playing", and follows Move's clock instead.
- A colour pad let go during a recording no longer stays on.
- A colour tapped off a chord stays off for the next chord, even with your finger still on the pad.
- With out-of-key chords hidden, pressing a dark pad keeps the colours on the chord that's sounding.
- Bloom no longer presses Move's track buttons while Schwung isn't reading Move (for example during a Set load).

The new control scheme is in the [0.11.0 notes](https://github.com/bondobd/schwung-bloom/releases/tag/v0.11.0).
