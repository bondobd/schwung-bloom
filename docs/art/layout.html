THE SURFACE

Every pad is a chord in your key

ColourHold while playing: Sus2, Sus4, Dim, Aug, 7, Maj7, 6, Add9. Blue keeps the chord in key, rose adds an outside note.
Strum plateThe held chord's notes, low to high. Brush across it.
Minor chordsOn the same roots. Diminished where the key has it.
Major chordsRoot to octave of the Set's key. Out-of-key pads go dark (or dim, if you let them play).
CAPTUREA fresh 4-chord progression in your key. Shift for 8.
WHEELA new take on every chord: 7ths, 9ths, sus, inversions, bass notes.
HOLD A STEPKnobs set how that chord plays: mode, length, gate, volume, bass.
PLAYMove and your progression start together. Shift: chords only.
LOOPHold it and tap two steps to loop just those.
RECORDChords onto your chord track, then bass onto your bass track.
